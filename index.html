About the game

Smart Cricket Game was built from the ground up for blind and visually impaired players — not adapted afterwards. If it happens in real cricket, it happens here: real teams, real grounds and the real laws of the game.

Designed to be heard

  • TalkBack-first design: big buttons, clear labels, and everything important is spoken aloud.
  • Nothing is visual-only: every score, decision and event is announced by voice — nothing important is only shown on screen.
  • Real cricket, faithfully: powerplay rules, DRS, the follow-on, declarations, Super Overs — the laws of the game are followed properly.
  • Your match, your choices: your own matches are never simulated without your tap. Where a choice exists, you make it.

Sighted players can play too

The game also has a full visual layer: a live scorebug, a field map showing where your fielders are placed, and a pitch strip showing where each ball pitched. Blind and sighted friends can follow the same match together.

Getting started: sign up & log in

You need a free account to play. It takes a minute, and it keeps your rewards safe in the cloud.

  1. Create your account

    Sign up with your email address and a strong password — at least 8 characters, with upper-case and lower-case letters, a number and a symbol. Or skip the password and use Google Sign-In.

  2. Collect your welcome bonus

    Every new account receives a welcome bonus of 500 coins, 500 silver, 500 gold and 500 diamonds — paid straight into your wallet.

  3. Stay logged in — permanently

    Once you log in, you stay logged in while the app is installed. You only need to log in again if you log out yourself, delete your account, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app.

  4. Forgot your password?

    Tap Forgot Password on the login screen and a reset link will be sent to your email. Follow it, choose a new password, and you are back in.

How to play a match — step by step

From the Home screen to the final scorecard, here is exactly how a match unfolds. Everything below is spoken aloud in the game as it happens.

  1. Choose your cricket

    From Home, choose Single Play, Leagues, Tournaments or Series. Then pick the two teams — there are 20 international teams, each with a named squad.

  2. Set the match and the venue

    Choose the match length: 2, 5, 10, 15 or 20 overs in Single Play, or 50 overs in ODI mode. Then pick the venue from 55+ real grounds in 20 countries — the pitch at each ground affects how the match plays.

  3. The toss — you always call

    At the toss, you always make the call. Both playing elevens are announced aloud before the match begins.

  4. Edit your squad any time

    In the Squad Editor you can edit your squad and choose your playing eleven whenever you like.

  5. Set your field

    Place your fielders where you want them. Real powerplay rules are enforced: in T20 cricket, a maximum of 2 fielders outside the circle in overs 1–6, and a maximum of 5 after that.

  6. Face each ball

    When you bat, you first hear the bowler’s delivery and its speed, and where the ball is pitching — the line is announced in truthful bands, such as “Just outside off stump.” Then you choose, in order:

    • Your movement, including the shuffle across the crease
    • Your batting technique for this ball
    • Your shot
    • The shot area

    Or you can Leave the ball and let it go through.

  7. Run between the wickets

    Running is in your hands. You hear the nearest fielder’s name and how far away he is, then choose how many runs to attempt — 0, 1, 2 or 3. Judge it well: a run out is always possible.

  8. The result and the scorecard

    After every ball you hear commentary and, when you bat, an honest analysis of your shot selection and timing. At the end you get the full result, the Man of the Match, and a complete scorecard you can copy and share.

Your batting set-up

Every batsman has a set-up you control. Set it when he arrives at the crease, and change it between overs or before any ball:

  • Guard: Leg, Middle-and-Leg, Middle, Middle-and-Off, or Off
  • Stance: Side-On or Open
  • Crease position: On the crease, Deep, or Out of the crease
  • Trigger movement: Still, Forward Press, Back and Across, or Double
  • Grip: V grip or Bottom-Hand
  • Backlift: Low or High

Technique on every ball

For each delivery you can play Normal, Down on One Knee, Give Yourself Room, Soft Hands or Hard Hands — each with real trade-offs, just like real batting.

28 real shots & a full delivery library

Drives, cuts, pulls, sweeps, the ramp, the helicopter shot and many more — 28 real shots in all. Bowlers answer with a full library of real deliveries: the yorker, the bouncer, the doosra, the flipper, slower balls and more.

Real extras and real laws

Wides — including chased wides you can score from — no balls you can hit, free hits, byes and leg byes. And all 8 ways of getting out: bowled, caught, LBW, run out, stumped, caught behind, hit wicket and more.

DRS — decision review system

Each team has 2 reviews per innings. Reviews are decided with the voices of the third umpire and the TV umpire, just like a real broadcast.

Game modes

One-off matches, full leagues, world tournaments, series and five-day Test cricket — every format of the real game is here.

Single Play

One match, your way. T20 or ODI, any two of the 20 international teams, at any of the 55+ grounds. Perfect for a quick game.

Leagues

10 full leagues, each run as a complete tournament: PSL, IPL, BBL, CPL, SA20, ILT20, BPL, LPL, MLC and The Hundred — played in its real 100-ball format.

Tournaments

The biggest stages in world cricket: the T20 World Cup 2024, the ODI World Cup 2023, the Asia Cup, the Champions Trophy 2025, the World Test Championship and the Hong Kong Sixes.

Series

Bilateral series of 1 to 7 matches in any format — T20, ODI or Test — plus tri-nation series. You choose the host country, and you pick the ground for every match yourself.

Test matches

Five days, three sessions a day, declarations, the follow-on and draws. After 80 overs the fielding captain can take the new ball — choosing one of four balls from the umpire’s box.

Super Overs & save and resume

Tied knockout matches are decided by a Super Over. Every match you play can be saved and resumed later. Robot-vs-robot fixtures can be simulated with one tap — but your own matches are never simulated without your tap.

Commentary & sounds

The game talks to you like a broadcast. Commentary is in spoken English throughout, and you can make it sound exactly how you want.

Commentary on every ball

Full spoken commentary on every delivery, plus broadcast-style commentary with an expert commentator voice at the big moments — wickets, milestones and the finish of a match.

Four voices, your choice

Separate voices you can set in Settings: the main commentator, the third umpire, the TV umpire and the expert commentator. Each one can use a different speech engine, and you can test them all before you play.

Sound slots & the crowd

Attach your own audio clips to 39 game events — a four, a six, a wicket, a fifty, a century, hit wicket, the crowd and many more. A random attached clip plays; if none is attached, the spoken commentary is used. A continuous crowd ambience runs underneath and swells at the big moments.

Make it yours: settings & profile

Voices, grounds, even your own shots — the game bends around how you want to play.

Settings

  • Choose the speech engine for each of the four voices, and test them.
  • Set the bowling speed.
  • Turn broadcast commentary and crowd sound on or off.

My Venues

Add your own grounds. Your venues join the venue lists everywhere in the game — including when you host a series.

My Creations

Add your own shots and deliveries. Only shots and deliveries recognised in real cricket history are accepted — so the game always stays true to real cricket.

Backup & restore

Export everything — settings, squads, venues, creations and sounds — into one backup file, and import it on a new phone. Nothing is lost when you change devices.

Your profile

From your Profile you can reach everything about your account:

  • My Rewards — your coin, silver, gold and diamond balances.
  • Settings — voices, bowling speed and sound options.
  • About & Support — about the game, the privacy policy and how to get help.
  • Change Password — update your password.
  • Delete Account — permanently delete your account and its data.
  • Logout — sign out of this device.

Help & FAQ

Answers to the questions players ask most. Each question opens to reveal its answer.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Tap Forgot Password on the login screen. A password reset link will be sent to your email address — follow it to choose a new password, then log in as normal.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Inside the game, go to Profile → Delete Account. You can also read the full instructions on the Account Deletion page.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The game is completely free, and its in-game currencies can only be earned by playing — they cannot be bought and have no real-money value.

What data does the game store?

Only two things are stored online: your account email address and your wallet balances. Everything else — your settings, squads, venues, creations and sounds — stays on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.

Can sighted players play?

Yes. Alongside the full voice commentary, the game has a visual layer with a live scorebug, a field map and a pitch strip showing where each ball pitched — so sighted players can play and follow along too.

Why do I stay logged in?

By design: once you log in, you stay logged in while the app is installed, so you can open the game and play straight away. You are only asked to log in again after logging out yourself, deleting the account, clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.
